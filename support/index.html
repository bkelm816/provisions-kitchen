What Provisions does

Plan the week's meals with leftovers built in, so each recipe is made in the right amount. Provisions turns the plan into one grocery list, combined across recipes and in the order you walk the store, and estimates what it costs at your Harris Teeter. Cooking mode walks you through each recipe with timers on the Lock Screen. You can share it all with your household.

Prices

  • Prices are for Harris Teeter only. Pick your store in Settings → Store with a ZIP code. Outside Harris Teeter's area, everything else works without prices.
  • They're estimates before discounts and tax, from Kroger's public product data. Sale prices need a VIC card.
  • Wrong product or price? On the List tab, choose Change product… on the item's row and pick another. If a price looks wrong at the store, email me the item, the store and the date.

Sharing with your household

  1. Each person needs their own Apple ID, with iCloud turned on.
  2. In Settings → Household, tap Invite someone and send the invitation.
  3. The other person opens it on their iPhone. Your recipes, plan and list then stay in step on both phones.

Without iCloud, Provisions works fully on one phone.

Importing recipes

  • From a screenshot: Recipes → + → From screenshot. On devices with Apple Intelligence, it reads the recipe on the phone. On others, it splits the text into ingredients and steps by rules, and you check them before saving.
  • From a link: Recipes → + → From a link, or in Safari, tap Share and choose Provisions.

Deleting your data

Deleted recipes go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. To remove everything from iCloud: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Provisions → Delete Data. See the privacy policy for what's stored where.
